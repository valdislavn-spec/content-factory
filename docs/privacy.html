Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how the Content Factory news network (“we”, “our”, “the project”) processes information in connection with our Telegram channels, the public hub website at valdislavn-spec.github.io/content-factory, and our Pinterest publishing application.

Short version: we publish news summaries. We do not create user accounts, do not sell personal data, and do not collect sensitive information. The only visitor analytics on our website is Google Analytics 4 (cookies). Messages you send to our Telegram contact are used only to answer you.

1. Who we are

The Content Factory is an independent news-summary project that publishes short, sourced summaries of publicly available news to Telegram channels in English and Spanish, and distributes related pins on Pinterest. Contact: Telegram @Novyi0007Bot or GitHub Issues.

2. Information we process

  • Website visitors. When you open our website we use Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated usage information: pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP, device/browser type, referral source and interactions. This uses cookies and similar technologies. We do not ask you to register and we do not intentionally collect names, emails or precise addresses.
  • Telegram channels and bot. Channel subscriptions are handled by Telegram; we do not receive subscribers’ phone numbers or account details. If you write to our contact bot or account, we see the message and your Telegram display name/handle, and use them only to reply and, if relevant, to discuss partnerships or advertising.
  • Pinterest. We publish pins through the official Pinterest API using our own application credentials. We do not receive information about Pinterest users who view or save our pins; Pinterest processes that data under its own Privacy Policy.
  • Third-party data we use for content. We read public RSS feeds and market-data APIs (e.g. CoinGecko, Kalshi) to prepare summaries. These requests are not tied to any website visitor or reader.

3. Cookies

Our website itself sets no cookies for accounts or advertising. Google Analytics 4 may set analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to distinguish sessions and measure traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; standard opt-out add-ons for Google Analytics are also available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

4. Why we process it (legal bases)

  • Legitimate interest in understanding aggregate website traffic and improving our content.
  • Consent where required by applicable law for analytics cookies; you can withdraw it at any time by blocking cookies.
  • Performance of a request when you contact us and we reply.

5. Third parties

  • Google (Analytics) — website traffic measurement.
  • Telegram — channel hosting and messaging.
  • Pinterest — pin distribution.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

6. Retention

Google Analytics data is retained according to our property settings (aggregated reports, limited retention period). Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation, then deleted or kept in ordinary correspondence archives without further use.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/EEA, UK GDPR, CCPA in California), you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. Because we do not maintain user accounts, we often cannot identify a website visitor; in that case we will explain what we can and cannot do. To exercise a right, contact us via @Novyi0007Bot and describe your request.

8. Children

Our content is general news and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Some channels cover sports betting and are marked 18+.

9. International transfers

Our providers (Google, Telegram, Pinterest, GitHub) are global services and may process data in countries other than yours, under their own safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when our practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated; continued use of our website and channels means you accept the current version.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: Telegram @Novyi0007Bot or GitHub Issues.
