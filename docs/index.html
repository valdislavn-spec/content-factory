FAQ

what readers ask

What is Content Factory?

A small news desk that publishes six Telegram channels: crypto, AI and US sports in English, plus the same three beats in Spanish.

How often are the channels updated?

Each channel gets 15–18 posts a day, scheduled through the day — not one bulk dump at midnight.

Where do the stories come from?

CoinDesk, The Block, Decrypt, TechCrunch, MIT Technology Review, ESPN and regional Spanish outlets. Every post links to the original source.

Are the channels free?

Yes. No paywall, no locked posts; support is voluntary and never required to read.
