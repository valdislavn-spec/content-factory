Luggage storage guide

Late flight but morning check-out? Luggage storage costs $1.9–6 per bag per day and turns a dead afternoon into a city walk. Here is how to find a safe spot.

Where to store

Main train stations, central cafes and dedicated storage shops. Book online to lock the price and skip the queue.

Prices

$1.9–6 per bag per day depending on the city and bag size. Cheaper than a late check-out in most hotels.

Safety

Choose providers with insurance and 24/7 access, keep valuables on you, photograph the receipt.

Timing

Store right after check-out, pick up 2–3 hours before your flight — leave margin for the airport transfer.
