Airport transfer guide

An airport transfer with a fixed price removes the classic arrival stress: no haggling, no meter surprises, no queue at midnight. Here is how to book one properly.

When to book

Book 1–3 days ahead. For early-morning flights, book the night before to lock the price and the driver.

What's included

Check: meet-and-greet at arrivals, flight tracking, waiting time, child seats, luggage capacity. These are the usual extra fees.

Price vs taxi

Fixed transfer is usually 10–30% cheaper than an airport taxi for 2+ people, and the price does not change in traffic.

Cancellation

Good providers allow free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup — check before paying.
