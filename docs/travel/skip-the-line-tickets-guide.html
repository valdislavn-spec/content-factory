Skip-the-line tickets

Skip-the-line is worth it at 4–5 attractions in Europe and a waste of money at the rest. Here is the simple rule to decide before you book.

Worth it

Colosseum, Vatican Museums, Eiffel Tower, Anne Frank House, Sagrada Família: queues are 1–3 hours without timed entry.

Not worth it

Small museums, churches and neighbourhood sights: queues are short and the premium is 30–50%.

City passes

Only pay off if you plan 3+ paid sights in 2–3 days. Do the maths before buying — transport is often not included.

Book ahead

2–4 weeks ahead for the big sights in high season. Same-day tickets are resold at a markup.
