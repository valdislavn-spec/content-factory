Car rental without surprises

Car rental looks cheap until the counter adds insurance, fuel and deposit holds. Here is how to book at the right time and avoid the classic traps.

When to book

2–3 weeks ahead is usually the sweet spot. Last-minute prices spike in summer; too early can be more expensive than waiting.

Insurance

Check your card's rental cover and the excess amount. Third-party excess insurance is often cheaper than the counter upsell.

Fuel and deposit

Choose full-to-full fuel policy. The deposit is usually blocked on your card — check the amount before booking.

Check the car

Photograph every scratch before driving off and return with the same fuel level.
