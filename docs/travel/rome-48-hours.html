48 hours in Rome

Two days is enough for Rome if you book the two big sights ahead and walk the rest. This route avoids the worst queues and the tourist-trap restaurants.

Day 1 — ancient Rome

Colosseum at 8:30 with a timed ticket, then Forum and Palatine Hill. Lunch near Monti. Afternoon: Pantheon, Piazza Navona, Trevi Fountain. Dinner in Trastevere.

Day 2 — Vatican and centro

Vatican Museums first entry (8:00–8:30) or St. Peter's Basilica early. Afternoon: Castel Sant'Angelo, Spanish Steps, Borghese Gardens.

Tickets

Book Colosseum and Vatican 2–4 weeks ahead. Skip-the-line is worth it at the Colosseum; city passes only pay off if you plan 3+ sights.

Food

Away from the main squares: pizza al taglio $4–6, pasta $12–16, gelato $3–4. Avoid restaurants with photo menus.
