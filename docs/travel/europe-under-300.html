Travel guide

Europe for under $300

A 3-night European city break for under $300 is possible if you pick the right month, fly carry-on only and book 6–8 weeks ahead. Here is the exact playbook we use.

When to go

March–May and late September–November are the cheapest windows. Avoid school holidays, Easter week and August. Tuesday and Wednesday departures are usually 20–35% cheaper than Friday–Sunday.

Flights

Book 6–8 weeks ahead. Use flexible-date search and compare nearby airports. Carry-on only: budget airlines charge more for a checked bag than for the ticket on short routes.

Where to stay

Central hostels and 3-star hotels 1–2 metro stops from the centre cut costs by a third. Book refundable rates early, then re-check prices two weeks before the trip.

Daily budget

Coffee and bakery breakfast: $5–7. Lunch at a market or local canteen: $10–14. Dinner: $15–20. Public transport day pass: $5–8.
