Frequently asked questions

Destinations and seasons are checked on 2026-11-13; prices change daily.

What's included in these packages?

Flight and hotel. Transfers, meals and insurance depend on the rate you pick — each partner page lists exactly what's covered.

How long is the flight from New York?

From 4 hours nonstop to Cancún and Punta Cana, up to 14 hours to Tokyo. One-stop options (Lisbon, Bali) take longer.

When is the cheapest time to travel?

Shoulder months are usually cheaper: May–June and September–October for Europe, May–November for the Caribbean. Each card lists the best months for that destination.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your passport and destination. US passport holders don't need a visa for Mexico, the Dominican Republic, the UK or the EU for short trips, but rules change — verify before booking.

Are the promo codes verified?

Yes — we check them on the partner's own page and record the date. Codes can expire or change, so the final terms are always shown at checkout.

Do you show live prices?

No. We show destinations, flight times and seasons, then send you to a live search page where current prices are shown for your dates.
