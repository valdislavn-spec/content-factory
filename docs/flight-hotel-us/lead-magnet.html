Book smarter: 7-point checklist

  1. Search ±3 days. A flexible-date search often beats a fixed-date search by 20–40%.
  2. Fly Tue–Wed. Midweek departures are usually cheaper than Friday or Sunday.
  3. Book the right window. Domestic: 4–8 weeks ahead. International: 6–12 weeks ahead.
  4. Carry-on only. Baggage fees can erase the fare difference.
  5. Check the airport. Secondary airports can be cheaper — factor in transfer time and cost.
  6. Pre-book the transfer. A fixed-price airport ride avoids surge pricing on arrival.
  7. Get an eSIM before you fly. Roaming passes are usually more expensive than a local data plan.
